About me

I'm Tzu-Hsiang Huang (or Jimmy, either works). I'm a second-year Ph.D. student in Computer Science at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign (UIUC), advised by Prof. Dakshita Khurana. My research interests lie in quantum cryptography and quantum complexity theory. Previously, I received my B.S. in Computer Science from National Taiwan University (NTU), where I was advised by Prof. Kai-Min Chung.
